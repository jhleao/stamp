## Design Context

### Users

Small teams on macOS making written documents, decks, and spreadsheets with a
mix of direct editing and agent help. They use Studio during quick iteration to
see what changed, inspect the output, resolve a conflict, and push a shared
version without learning the implementation.

### Brand Personality

Cool, precise, and low-ceremony. Stamp should feel like a finely made data
workbench: fast under the hand, quiet while work is happening, and candid when
something needs attention.

### Aesthetic Direction

A compact technical workbench influenced by the supplied database application:
cool graphite and mineral-white surfaces, crisp one-pixel dividers, compact
square controls, restrained burnt-orange state accents, and a purposeful sans/mono type
pair. Light and dark modes are equally first-class. Avoid warm cream surfaces,
generic SaaS cards, gradients, glowing chrome, and gratuitous rounding.

### Design Principles

- Keep the document visually dominant; interface chrome recedes.
- Make state legible in plain language: local, rendering, conflict, pushed.
- Prefer one decisive action over rows of controls.
- Use cool neutrals, sharp structure, and color only for meaning.
- Use ordinary browser and platform behavior before adding widgets.
- Every frontend dependency and visual flourish must justify its footprint.
